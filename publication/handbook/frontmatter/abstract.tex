\chapter*{About This Handbook}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About This Handbook}

This handbook explains the modular detector-controller architecture as a coherent system story for technically competent readers who are not already familiar with the project.

Resolved Architecture Decision Records in the source repository remain authoritative. All figures and tables are explanatory and non-normative unless explicitly identified otherwise. This handbook is a derived, non-normative explanation and does not replace ADRs, interface-control documents, board-design specifications, or operating procedures.

The project originated at NOIRLab. Official project documents provide organizational motivation and historical context; resolved ADRs control the current architecture when earlier descriptions differ.

The system concept guide in \texttt{integration/system\_concept\_guide.md} is the maintained explanatory content source. Handbook chapters expand it and are kept aligned manually; shared reference tables are generated from their authoritative sources.
