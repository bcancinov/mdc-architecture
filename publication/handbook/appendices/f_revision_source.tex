\section{Source Hierarchy}

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\bfseries\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.29\linewidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X@{}}
\toprule
Repository area & Role \\
\midrule
\texttt{decisions/} & Resolved ADRs containing the current cross-system architectural decisions \\
\texttt{interfaces/} & Planned ICDs containing exact boundary definitions and implementation-specific values \\
\texttt{design/} & Planned board hardware and firmware design specifications \\
\texttt{integration/} & Explanatory and integration material \\
\texttt{publication/} & Derived LaTeX handbook sources, generators, diagrams, and build tooling \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

The version and generation date identify the PDF. For a controlled release, the approved PDF and its matching repository snapshot are archived under the same formal document version.
